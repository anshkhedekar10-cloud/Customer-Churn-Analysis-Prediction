# Machine Learning Model Evaluation – Customer Churn Prediction

## 1. Model Overview

Two Machine Learning models were developed and evaluated to predict customer churn.

* Model 1: Original Model
* Model 2: Balanced Model

The objective was to identify customers who are likely to leave the telecom company.

## 2. Model Performance Comparison

| Evaluation Metric | Original Model | Balanced Model |
| ----------------- | -------------: | -------------: |
| Accuracy          |         80.31% |         72.71% |
| Precision         |         64.56% |         49.17% |
| Recall            |         57.49% |         79.41% |
| F1-Score          |         60.82% |         60.74% |

## 3. Understanding the Results

### Original Model

* Achieved higher accuracy of 80.31%.
* Demonstrated better precision of 64.56%.
* Identified 57.49% of actual churn customers.
* Suitable when reducing false positive predictions is important.

### Balanced Model

* Achieved 72.71% accuracy.
* Improved recall to 79.41%.
* Identified a larger proportion of customers who actually churn.
* Suitable when identifying as many potential churn customers as possible is the priority.

## 4. Model Selection

The Balanced Model is preferred for customer retention purposes because it achieves higher recall.

Although its accuracy is lower, it identifies more customers who are likely to leave.

This allows the business to take early action through targeted retention campaigns.

However, its lower precision means the company may contact more customers who would not actually churn.

## 5. Business Impact

The Machine Learning model can help businesses:

* Identify customers at risk of leaving.
* Develop targeted retention strategies.
* Improve customer engagement.
* Reduce customer loss.
* Support data-driven business decisions.

## 6. Conclusion

The Balanced Model achieved a recall of 79.41%, compared with 57.49% for the Original Model.

For a customer retention strategy where missing potential churners is costly, the Balanced Model is a reasonable choice.

Further tuning and validation can improve the model's predictive performance.
