# Business Insights – Customer Churn Analysis

## 1. Key Findings

1. Contract Type: Customers with month-to-month contracts show higher churn compared with customers on longer-term contracts.

2. Customer Tenure: Customers with shorter tenure are more likely to leave, indicating that early customer engagement is important.

3. Internet Service: Fiber optic customers represent an important segment to investigate for potential churn-related issues.

4. Senior Citizens: Senior citizens demonstrate a higher churn rate than non-senior customers.

5. Partner Status: Customers without partners show higher churn rates.

6. Dependents: Customers without dependents are more likely to leave.

7. Gender: Churn rates are relatively similar between male and female customers.

## 2. Business Recommendations

* Introduce loyalty programs for long-term customers.
* Offer special discounts to month-to-month customers who switch to annual contracts.
* Develop onboarding programs for new customers.
* Investigate service quality and pricing concerns among fiber optic customers.
* Provide personalized retention offers to high-risk customer segments.
* Improve customer support and engagement.

## 3. Business Objective

The objective is to reduce customer churn, improve customer satisfaction, increase customer retention, and support data-driven business decisions.
